# Community guidelines

NavZip welcomes contributors of different backgrounds, experience levels and viewpoints. A useful discussion stays respectful, specific and centered on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and patient with users and contributors.
- Explain technical disagreement with evidence and actionable suggestions.
- Accept corrections and respect the time of volunteer maintainers.
- Keep discussions accessible: use English, provide context and avoid unnecessary jargon.
- Respect privacy, licenses and the rights of others.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content directed at people, doxxing, deliberate intimidation, impersonation and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable. Do not post private credentials, profiles or personal information.

## Moderation

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, reject contributions or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and protect affected participants. Repeated or severe misconduct may result in exclusion.

Use GitHub's report/abuse tools for platform violations. For non-sensitive project conduct concerns, contact the maintainer through an available contact on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/LuisPeixoto); avoid posting private details in public threads. Security vulnerabilities follow the separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).

These guidelines apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other project-managed spaces. They are the project's own guidelines and do not claim affiliation with a third-party code-of-conduct program.
